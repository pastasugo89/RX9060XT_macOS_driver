## First bare-metal headless Metal tests

The user approved the extension and rebooted. The AuxKC contained the extension but `showloaded` initially did not list it. A subsequent explicit `kmutil load -p` succeeded and `showloaded` listed `com.navi48.accelprobe 0.0.7`. Publishing the Metal nub with `n48nub publish` succeeded. `mtlprobe list` identified one headless `AMD Radeon RX 9060 XT` using `Navi48Device`; the same 81 missing class/selector pairs were reported as in P0.

Initial `qccompute`, `compute`, and `quad` probes hit missing SPIR-V cache entries. Their generated AIR and sidecars were copied into a user-owned isolated workspace; the staged translator ran as uid 501 with sandbox enabled and the actual `spirv-val`. With `N48M_TEST_SIDE_DIR` pointing at that isolated output, `qccompute` passed creation of QuartzCore compute pipelines (it does not dispatch them); `compute` dispatched and compared buffer/texture results with zero mismatches; `quad` rendered 256x256 off-screen and compared all 65,536 pixels exactly, zero differences. Logs and translated artifacts are in `research/hw_dump/macos_stage17_run_20261027/`. No translation daemon was installed or started, and the runtime test cache is not a production cache. The nub was published manually for this boot. These results establish limited headless Metal operation, not accelerated WindowServer, application compatibility, sustained stability, thermal safety, or a functioning iGPU display fallback.
