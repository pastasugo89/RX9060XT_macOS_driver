## Measured hot path and prepared bundle25

Five-second samples of live WindowServer608 and Warp880 were captured locally. In the WindowServer main-thread tree,91samples reach dladdr from n48s_classify,87of them in dyld findClosestSymbol, through repeated CoreAnimation IOSurface wrapper creation. The classification code only caches positive surface decisions: a negative first caller must not prevent a later CoreDisplay caller from recognizing that same surface. Caching negative IOSurface decisions would risk breaking presentation.

Added a separate256entry bounded cache of symbol predicates keyed by exact return PC, exclusively for Mach-O images marked MH_DYLIB_IN_CACHE. Their shared-cache mappings have immutable PC meanings for the process lifetime. Ordinary unloadable dylib results remain uncached; all accesses are serialized by the existing N48S.mu. Cache collisions compare the complete PC and miss safely. Negative *surface* decisions still retry from every caller; positive cached symbol matches can stop walking the remaining frames when no diagnostic stack dump is requested. The first diagnostic dumps retain full symbol logging. Corrected the stale comment claiming negative surface verdicts were cached.
